Logo concepts for Manafe3 (منافع) — PEB design, detailing and fabrication. Six symbol ideas share one custom wordmark: the two A's are gable portal frames and the 3 is a mirrored E — it is also the ع of منافع. Everything is drawn as geometry, so it needs no font. Concepts only: pick one (or mix) before it goes into the app.

1 · Symbol concepts — each shown in a horizontal lockup, light and dark

A · Tapered Frame RECOMMENDED

A tapered PEB portal frame — narrow bases, deep haunches, shallow apex — with two bays receding behind it on the slab. It says exactly what Manafe3 does (tapered built-up frames, Phase 1) and reads as a building from far away.

B · M-Frame

The letter M drawn as a two-span tapered frame with a valley. The boldest shape — best for a tiny icon, a stamp on steel, or embroidery.

C · Nut & I-Section

A hex nut around a welded I-section: the bolt and the beam — connections and members. Close to the steel-trade logos you sent; very "fabricator".

D · Isometric Bay

Two frames joined by purlins in 3D — the BIM model. Leans to "software" rather than "workshop".

E · Grid Bubble

A drawing grid marker that reads M3, with its chain line. Speaks to draftsmen and detailers; works as a favicon.

F · Seal

An emblem for calc sheets, drawing title blocks, certificates and social posts — not the main logo.

The name

  • The 3 stands for ع (Arabizi) — always in orange, so the name reads in both worlds.
  • Both A's are portal frames: the wordmark is a row of buildings.
  • Short forms: M3 for the icon, file extension .m3peb or .manafe3 for project files.
  • Module names in the same voice: Manafe3 Frame, Purlin, Connect, Draw, Cut (NC).

Uses in the app

  • Favicon and app-bar mark (replaces the blue "M3" placeholder).
  • Splash screen with the erection animation while the engine starts.
  • Header of every HTML/PDF calc sheet and MTO, and the drawing title block.
  • The red seal as the automatic NOT FOR ISSUE watermark until checks are verified; switch to the navy seal when they are.
  • Report colours: orange = governing check, navy = pass, red = fail.

Outside the app

  • Reel / LinkedIn end card: the loader animation, then the lockup.
  • Windows installer icon and desktop shortcut (Phase 7) from the 256 px tile.
  • Letterhead, business card, email signature with the bilingual lockup.
  • Laser-cut steel plate of concept B for the office wall or trade-show stand.
  • Website hero: the isometric bay (D) drawn in line, assembling on scroll.
